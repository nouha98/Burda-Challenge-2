# Image style for das schmeckt! – derived from the brand guide's cover performance pages

Source: `challenge-2/references/MarkenCI_das schmeckt.pdf`, p. 4 "Top 10 Cover" (CPI +9.4 % to +22.2 %) and
p. 5 "Low 10 Cover" (CPI −9.9 % to −24.6 %), compared visually on 2026-11-20. The top covers are the template;
the low covers show what to avoid. These are style rules, not copies: the cover photos are not passed to the image model.

## What the top covers have in common (do this)
- Hearty, warm, filling dishes with **golden-brown, roasted, seared or crisp surfaces** and visible sauce; generous,
  abundant portions where every component is recognisable ("fressig", "macht satt und glücklich").
- The food is shown **in the vessel it is cooked or served in**: roasting dish, gratin/baking dish, frying pan,
  cast-iron or enamel pot, or a large white serving bowl/platter – often with **a serving spoon, fork or cake server
  resting in the food**.
- **High camera angle (about 60–75°) or straight top-down**, the dish fills most of the frame and the vessel's
  edges are cut off by the frame; the food is the hero.
- **Calm, light or warm neutral background**: light or warm wood, white, pale grey or soft grey-blue; at most a
  linen or checked napkin edge or a small side bowl partly in frame.
- **Warm colour palette**: golden, brown, cream, tomato red, with green only as a small accent.
- Bright, soft natural daylight, crisp detail, true colours.

## What the low covers have in common (avoid this)
- Salads, raw vegetables and cold dishes dominating the frame; **large green areas**.
- Sweet berry cakes and fruit tarts as the main motif.
- **Strongly coloured backgrounds** (purple, turquoise, pink, red) or dark moody scenes.
- Pale food without browning; busy props.

## Matching hypotheses from the brand guide (p. 3)
Baking/sweets and salads perform worse; too much green performs worse; meat dishes and classic dishes perform well.
